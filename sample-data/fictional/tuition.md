# Fictional tuition assistance policy

This policy is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's rules.

## Tuition assistance eligibility

Full-time employees become eligible for tuition assistance after six months of employment. Eligible employees may request assistance for a course related to their current work or an agreed professional development plan.

## Part-time tuition eligibility

Part-time staff are not eligible for tuition assistance. Completing six months of employment does not make a part-time employee eligible. The full-time employment requirement applies when the employee submits the request.

## Tuition assistance requests

An eligible employee submits the course description and estimated tuition to the learning coordinator before enrolling. The learning coordinator reviews the request against the professional development plan and available budget.
