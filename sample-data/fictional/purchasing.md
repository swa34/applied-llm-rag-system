# Fictional purchasing policy

This policy is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's rules.

## Purchasing approval

The department head approves purchase requests before an employee places an order. Each request must describe the item or service, its work purpose, the proposed supplier, and the estimated total cost. The department head checks the department budget and records the approval.

## Purchase records

After an approved purchase, the employee sends the receipt and the recorded department head approval to the purchasing coordinator. The purchasing coordinator keeps those records together for the department.
