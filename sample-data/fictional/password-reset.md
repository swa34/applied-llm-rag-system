# Fictional password reset guide

This guide is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; these steps do not refer to a real account or service.

## Self-service password reset

Open the fictional staff sign-in page and select "Forgot password?" Enter your staff email address and select "Send reset link." Open the reset email, follow its link, choose a new password, and return to the sign-in page to sign in with the new password.

## Password reset help

If the reset email does not arrive, check your spam folder and confirm that you entered your staff email address correctly. If you cannot access that email account, contact the fictional IT service desk for account recovery assistance.
