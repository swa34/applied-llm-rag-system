# Fictional community studio booking policy

This policy is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's rules.

The statements in this policy apply to the fall program term.

## Reservation approval

A studio reservation requires approval from the program director at least ten business days before the event.

## Capacity and refreshments

The studio's maximum capacity is 40 people. Sealed nonalcoholic drinks and packaged snacks are permitted. Open-flame warmers are prohibited.
