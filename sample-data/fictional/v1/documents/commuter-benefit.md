# Fictional commuter benefit guide

This guide is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's benefits.

## Eligibility and amount

Full-time and part-time staff may receive the commuter benefit. Northbridge contributes 60 percent of an eligible cost, up to a maximum contribution of $90 per month.

## Enrollment timing

An employee submits an enrollment request by the 20th day of a month for the benefit to begin the following month. A request submitted after the 20th begins one month later.

## Covered travel

The commuter benefit supports monthly public bus, rail, and ferry passes and bicycle-share memberships.
