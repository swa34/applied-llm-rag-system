# Fictional visitor access guide

This guide is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's access rules.

## Advance registration

A Northbridge host submits the visitor's name, company, and visit date at least one business day before the visit.

## Same-day arrival

A same-day visitor may enter only after the Safety Office confirms that the visitor's sponsor is available. The visitor must then sign in at the front desk.

## Hours and badges

The front desk handles routine visitor arrivals Monday through Friday from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. A visitor presents photo identification when receiving a badge and returns the badge when departing. This guide does not address weekend access.
