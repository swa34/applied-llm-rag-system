# Fictional caregiver leave policy

This policy is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's rules.

## Eligibility and allowance

Full-time staff become eligible for paid caregiver leave after 90 calendar days of employment. An eligible employee may use up to three paid caregiver days in a calendar year.

## Notice

An employee should notify their manager of caregiver leave as soon as practicable. When the need is unforeseen, the employee may give notice by the end of the next business day.

## Documentation

No documentation is required for one caregiver day. For two or more consecutive caregiver days, the employee submits an attendance confirmation to the Wellness Office. The confirmation must not include a diagnosis.
