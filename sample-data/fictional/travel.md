# Fictional travel policy

This policy is invented for the Northbridge Learning Institute demonstration. Northbridge Learning Institute is fictional; this document does not describe any real organization's rules.

## Overnight trip approval

An employee must obtain approval from their direct supervisor before booking an overnight work trip. The supervisor reviews the trip's work purpose, dates, and estimated cost. Approval is required before making a reservation.

## Part-time travel eligibility

Part-time staff may take approved work trips. Part-time staff follow the same overnight trip approval process as full-time staff: their direct supervisor approves the trip before booking. Employment status does not change who approves an overnight trip.

## Covered travel expenses

For an approved work trip, the travel policy covers reasonable transportation, lodging, and meals. Employees submit itemized receipts after returning. The reimbursement request must identify the approved trip and its work purpose.
